Move and resize the keyboard anywhere on screen. Layouts: QWERTY, AZERTY, German and symbols. Includes
autocomplete, clipboard history, text snippets (`/email` expands to your address), light, dark, nature
and sunset themes, and secure auto-fill with an option to disable logging on sensitive fields.
The AI assistant (bring your own OpenAI API key) can proofread, continue your text and suggest wording.

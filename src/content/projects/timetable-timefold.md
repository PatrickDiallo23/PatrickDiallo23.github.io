The system schedules teaching activities while respecting room limits, instructor availability and student
preferences, using evolutionary algorithms and multi-objective optimization. Published in
*Applied Sciences* (MDPI, 2024) and presented to the Luxoft Java community in a webinar. An earlier
paper on spatial indexing for emergency-response routes appeared at [CSCS 2023 (IEEE Xplore)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10214866/).
